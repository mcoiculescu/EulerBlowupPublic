# Frozen paper proof blueprint

The main Lean target is the frozen paper’s Theorem 1.1. Its quantifiers require
one smooth odd divergence-free datum on the unit three-torus and a maximal
classical unforced Euler trajectory with finite positive lifespan. The
conclusion includes both divergent time integrals, a uniform velocity
bound, central curl diverging as $t\uparrow T_*$, and $C^m(K)$ limits
of $u$ and $\partial_tu$ for every compact $K\subset\mathbb T^3\setminus\{0\}$
and every $m$. The stronger auxiliary smooth extension is tracked under
AUX. [Theorem 1.1 and the auxiliary remark](../frozen-manuscript/sections/01_problem.tex#L60)

## Statement correspondence

[`TorusEuler.Theorem11`](lean/TorusEuler/Statement.lean) uses spatial fields
on Euclidean three-space invariant under every integer lattice
translation. The spatial measure is Lebesgue measure restricted to
the centered unit cube. The velocity bound and the two integrands use
essential suprema. The nonnegative time integrals are extended-real
Lebesgue integrals over the half-open existence interval.

The data are smooth, periodic, odd, and divergence free. The velocity
agrees with the data at zero. The PDE uses a one-sided time derivative
there and the spatial Fréchet derivative for advection. The pressure is
smooth, periodic, and normalized by the convention in §1. A continuation
must solve the same PDE on a longer interval and agree with the velocity
throughout its original interval. The continuation predicate applies
regardless of the continuation's symmetry. The terminal filter is
nontrivial for every positive lifespan.

The origin is removed by taking the complement of the integer lattice.
For every compact of the punctured lift and each finite order, convergence
means uniform convergence of the genuine ambient spatial derivatives
through that order. Each limit has a witness of that regularity on an open
neighborhood of the compact. This formulation includes compacts across
cell seams. The two limits are specified for velocity and its time
derivative. The stronger auxiliary conclusion is tracked under AUX.

## Appendix C and the parameter estimates

Appendix C proves Lemma 1.3. The schedule keeps each current order
admissible and advances when the next threshold is reached.
`frequencySchedule_order_advance_iff` gives that criterion,
`frequencySchedule_three_log_steps` gives the product of three frequency
exponents, and `frequencySchedule_log_bound_of_maxima` gives the
resulting squared-logarithm bound. [Schedule proofs](lean/TorusEuler/FrequencySchedule.lean)
